# URUZ Flow V17 — Job Movement Engine

- Service-based workflow templates.
- Dashboard company names open Job Movement.
- Stage Handoff requires next-stage PIC and due date.
- Waiting/Blocked state keeps the job in the same department.
- Blocked duration is accumulated separately from processing flow.
- Every handoff/block/resume/close persists to the job document and auditLogs.
- Movement history is stored on each job for parcel-style traceability.
- Job closes only after its workflow template's final stage.
- Account & Tax: Sorting > Bank > Tax > Closing > ACC > Dispatch.
- Bookkeeping: Sorting > Bank > Closing > ACC > Dispatch.
